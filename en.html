Intervention and photographic series, 2026.
1 / 3

The work rephrases “WE SHOULD ALL BE FEMINISTS”, marketed by Dior since 2017 on luxury T-shirts costing around €750, as “WE SHOULD ALL WEAR DIOR”.

Some phrases change meaning depending on who says them. And on who wears them.

05

REGLA DE ARTISTA

2024 · Series of 32 pieces
1 / 4
Medium
Acrylic and metallic enamel on canvas.
Dimensions
45 × 50 cm per piece (unframed).

A journey around the world through 32 euphemisms and dysphemisms used to avoid saying “menstruation”.

Between humour, contempt, shame, poetry and superstition, the work examines the taboo that persists as cultural sediment in everyday language.

The series takes its formal references from Merda d’artistaby Piero Manzoni and Campbell’s Soup Cansby Andy Warhol.

About

INÉS DE SENÍS

Visual artist and podcaster

Inés de Senís is the artistic pseudonym of María José Ramudo, a visual artist and journalist based in Madrid. As a TVE correspondent in Jerusalem, Moscow and other locations, she covered conflicts and historical events including the Gulf War, the coup against Mikhail Gorbachev and the dissolution of the USSR. She was Head of Culture for TVE’s news programmes, Deputy Director of Informe Semanal and editor of La 2 Noticias. Her journalism has received several awards, including the Award for the Defence of Freedom of Expression from the Association of Foreign Correspondents.

Through irony and the appropriation of visual culture’s codes, her artistic practice explores the relationships between power, consumption, identity and representation through painting, photography, objects and installations.

She directs and hosts FLIPA SPAIN!, a video podcast about contemporary art and creativity in Spain, “and other ways of NOT understanding one another”.
